### 2d. Hindi vs English differences found on the same card (important)

1. English caption 4 = "Headache, blurring of vision, fits and swelling all over the body". Hindi caption 4 **adds "उल्टी" (vomiting)** and orders the items differently.
2. English caption 5 = "Labour pain before term/ Labour pain for more than 12 hours/Reduced fetal movment". Hindi caption 5 has **no "labour pain before term"**; it only has labour pain >12 hours and reduced fetal movement.
3. English caption 6 second half = "Preterm labour pains (<37 weeks)". Hindi says "समय पूर्व पानी की थैली का फटना (<37 सप्ताह पूर्व)" i.e. preterm **rupture of the water bag**, not preterm labour pains.
4. English heading says "nearest appropriate hospital"; Hindi heading says only "अस्पताल".
5. English newborn item "Severe chest indrawing"; Hindi "सांस लेते समय छाती का धंसना" has no word for "severe".

So Hindi sentences must be traced to the Hindi card, not translated from the English card.

- "convulsions."
- "severe headaches with blurred vision."
- "fever and too weak to get out of bed."
- "severe abdominal pain."
- "fast or difficult breathing."

- "fever."
- "abdominal pain."

## Cross-source disagreements worth knowing

1. **MCP card English vs Hindi** (same 2018 card): Hindi adds vomiting ("उल्टी"), drops "labour pain before term", and changes "preterm labour pains" to preterm rupture of the water bag. See 2d.
2. **Number of antenatal visits:** MCP card "Have at least 3 antenatal checkups, after registration" vs WHO PCPNC M2 "Visit the health centre at least 4 times during your pregnancy".
3. **Postnatal schedule:** MCP card / guidebook: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, 6th week (4 visits by ANM). HBNC (ASHA): Days 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42 (institutional) or 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42 (home). WHO 2022: within 24 h, 48–72 h, 7–14 days, week six.
4. **Newborn danger signs:** MCP card (7 items) closely matches WHO 2022 Rec. 25 but **omits jaundice** ("any jaundice in first 24 hours after birth, or yellow palms and soles at any age"); the guidebook mentions "yellow discoloration of the skin" only in a hint for ASHA. Temperature thresholds differ slightly: card "37.5º C or above" vs WHO 2022 "> 37.5 °C"; card "less than 35.5º C" matches. WHO PCPNC (2015) newborn lists additionally include bleeding, diarrhoea, "very small", and cord/eye/skin signs that the Indian card does not.
5. **Pregnancy danger signs:** WHO PCPNC lists "severe abdominal pain" and "fast or difficult breathing", which the MCP card does not list as such (the card has breathlessness only attached to severe anaemia). The MCP card lists reduced fetal movement and labour >12 hours; WHO puts those in the delivery sheet (M9) and chart C7, not in the C15/M2 pregnancy danger-sign list.
6. **Heavy bleeding definition (WHO, internal):** D28 "more than 2 or 3 pads soaked in 20-30 minutes after delivery" vs M9 "soaks more than 2-3 pads in 15 minutes".
7. **JSSK newborn coverage:** 30 days (2011 guideline) vs one year (2018 MCP card).
8. **IFA in pregnancy:** MCP card "for at least 6 months after first trimester. Take at least 180 tablets" vs AMB 2026 "for 180 days starting from the second trimester" (consistent in substance; wording differs).
