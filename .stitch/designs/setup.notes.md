I have created the **Add a person** screen for **AMMA**, designed specifically for low-literacy maternal checkups on shared family smartphones:

### Key Design Details:
- **Warm & Reassuring Tone:** Styled with AMMA's warm terracotta (`#E07A5F`), soft sand background, and rounded cards to create a comforting, "trusted aunt" presence.
- **Header:** Clean top bar with a simple text `Back` button, AMMA wordmark, and a language toggle indicator.
- **Honey Privacy Notice:** A warm amber/honey callout card highlighting: *"Anyone who can open this phone can see this record unless you set a PIN."* to safeguard privacy on shared family devices.
- **Visible, Accessible Form Fields:**
  - **Record Name:** Label clearly placed above, prefilled with *"Sunita"* (alongside Hindi script *"सुनीता"* badge).
  - **Language:** Selector set to *"हिंदी (Hindi)"*.
  - **Status Selection:** Two balanced choice chips with supportive icons — *"Pregnant"* (active/selected in terracotta) and *"Baby is born"* (unselected).
  - **Dates & Reminders:** Distinct white rounded cards with calendar icons for *"Expected date of birth"* and *"Next clinic visit"*.
  - **SMS Phone Number:** Dedicated field with reassurance note regarding the weekly check question.
  - **"What the nurse has told her":** High-contrast touch-friendly checkboxes for *"High blood pressure"* and *"First pregnancy"*.
  - **PIN Lock:** Optional 4-digit security field with padlock indicator.
- **Footer Actions:** Spacious bottom row with a text `Back` button and a prominent, rounded pill `Save` action button.
- **No distractions:** Excluded bottom navigation bars, charts, or unnecessary biometric widgets to keep the experience calm and focused.
