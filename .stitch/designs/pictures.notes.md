I have designed the **AMMA - Recall Danger Signs** mobile screen in portrait format, strictly adhering to the Warm Cradle design system and the UX needs of pregnant women and new mothers on shared devices:

### Design Highlights:
1. **Top Bar**:
   - Clean, text-accompanied **"Close"** action with clear target size.
   - Distinctive **"Replay"** button with a speaker icon and active visual feedback to listen to the prompt again.
   - Soft step badge indicating an active "Danger Check".

2. **Spoken Question Card**:
   - High-contrast, large-format question: *"Which signs mean you must go to the hospital straight away?"*
   - Bilingual subtitle (*"कौन से लक्षणों में तुरंत अस्पताल जाना जरूरी है?"*) for Hindi/Marathi/shared phone contexts.
   - Visual cue showing an urgent hospital badge and animated waveform voice indicator.

3. **Prominent "Tap and Speak" Voice Action**:
   - Located right above the grid with a terracotta circular mic button and audio halo, encouraging hands-free or voice responses.

4. **Two-Column Grid of 6 Large Picture Tiles**:
   - **Bleeding** (*खून बहना* / blood type drop symbol)
   - **Fits** (*दौरे / झटके* / electric bolt convulsion symbol)
   - **High fever** (*तेज़ बुखार* / thermometer symbol)
   - **Severe headache** (*तेज़ सिरदर्द* / neurology/head pain symbol)
   - **Baby not moving** (*बच्चे की हरकत बंद* / expectant mother symbol)
   - **Swollen hands** (*हाथों में सूजन* / raised hand symbol)
   - Each tile features high-contrast danger alert styling, large tactile touch targets, bilingual micro-labels, and interactive checkmarks for multi-selection.

5. **Quiet Fallback Input & Offline Reassurance**:
   - Discreet "Or type here..." keyboard input bar with an immediate "Send" arrow.
   - Reassurance footer noting that AMMA works offline and stays safely stored on the device.

Suggestions:
- Add an emergency confirmation drawer when danger tiles are selected
- Design the audio recording review state after voice input
- Switch the primary language between Hindi and English
