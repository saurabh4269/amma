Here is the screen for when a danger sign is detected in the **AMMA** weekly maternal health check:

### Key Design Highlights:
1. **Calm Urgency & High Clarity:**
   - The top banner uses a deliberate, high-contrast crimson/terracotta alert treatment (`Go to the hospital now / अस्पताल तुरंत जाएं`) alongside a prominent medical warning badge. It signals seriousness without alarming or causing blind panic.
   - The verbatim spoken audio transcript (*"You have one danger sign. Go to the nearest suitable hospital straight away"*) is presented prominently with Hindi text below and an accessible "Replay / बोलकर सुनें" control at the top.

2. **Action-Oriented Plan:**
   - **Her Action Plan card:** Displays key destination details clearly (`District Hospital, 12 km away, 24/7 Emergency`) and the local health worker contact (`Sunita Devi, ASHA Worker`).
   - **One-Tap Dialing:** A full-width, high-touch primary call button triggers a direct phone call to Sunita without complex steps.

3. **Shared Phone Accommodations:**
   - **SMS helper card:** A dedicated module allows drafting and sending an instant SMS summary to the mother's own basic feature phone, ensuring she retains the clinic guidance even after returning the shared family smartphone.

4. **Zero Distractions:**
   - Complies strictly with AMMA design principles: no confusing telemetry graphs, no login gate, no bottom nav bar, and an offline-ready local storage confirmation at the bottom.
