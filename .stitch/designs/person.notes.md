I've designed the profile and check-in screen for **Sunita** inside **AMMA**, tailored for low-stress maternal checks on shared family phones.

### Key Design Choices:
- **Calm, Reassuring Tone:** Built around the *AMMA Warm Cradle* design palette with warm cream tones, soft rounded cards, and welcoming terracotta accents designed to feel like advice from a trusted aunt.
- **Clear Information Hierarchy:** 
  - **Top Bar:** Simple left-aligned "Back" button and bold, clear header identifying Sunita's profile.
  - **Status Card:** Prominently highlights **"Pregnant · 28 weeks"** and last check-in date (`1 Oct`), alongside a reassuring **"PIN on"** privacy chip indicating her medical logs remain protected on a shared device.
  - **Thumb-Friendly Primary Action:** A full-width, high-contrast terracotta pill button reading **"Start this week's session"** that is immediately reachable and unambiguous.
  - **2x2 Action Grid:** Clean, white tactile cards with accessible line icons and paired descriptions for key helper tasks (*Card for the clinic*, *Teach the phone my voice*, *Send danger-sign audio*, and *Sources*).
  - **Subtle Utility Actions:** Quiet offline guarantee banner and a discreet **"Delete this record"** action placed at the bottom without cluttering primary health tasks.
