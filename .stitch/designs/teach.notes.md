Here is the **"Teach the phone my voice"** screen designed for AMMA, following the warm, reassuring visual language of the existing maternal health system:

### Key Design Highlights:
1. **Warm, Caring Atmosphere**:
   - Crafted with AMMA's soft palette (`#FAF7F2` linen background, `#E07A5F` terracotta accents, deep cocoa typography, and soft rounded cards) avoiding clinical harshness.
   - Includes privacy reassurance at the top and bottom emphasizing that recordings stay strictly on this device and work offline.

2. **Clear Information & Visual Scaffolding**:
   - Top navigation includes a clean back button and bilingual language toggle.
   - Prominent, easy-to-read guidance text: *"Tap a sign, say it in your own words, then tap again. Do each one two or three times."* accompanied by Hindi subtitles for low-literacy and multi-lingual comfort.

3. **Quiet vs. Active Visual Hierarchy**:
   - **Active Recording State (High Fever · 1 of 3 saved)**: Elevated with a terracotta outline, pulsating "LISTENING..." badge, prompt clue (*Say: "Mera shareer bohot garam hai"*), and a large, tactile button clearly labelled **"Tap when you have finished"**.
   - **Quiet States**: Clean, calm white cards displaying recognizable illustrated icons, clear danger sign labels, bilingual translations, and visual 3-dot progress indicators:
     - *Bleeding during pregnancy* (2 saved)
     - *Fits (convulsions)* (0 saved / Not recorded)
     - *Severe headache & blurry vision* (0 saved)
     - *Baby moving less* (3 saved · Ready ✓)
